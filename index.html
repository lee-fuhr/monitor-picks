How we choose our picks

We base every recommendation on three pillars: the brand’s long‑term reputation for reliability, the collective feedback of hundreds of owners who live with these monitors daily, and the conclusions of trusted third‑party reviewers who perform real‑world testing. We pay special attention to what actually breaks, dead pixels after a year, wobbly stands, flaky USB‑C hubs, not just peak brightness numbers from a lab. Value isn’t just the lowest price; it’s the monitor that serves you well for years without hidden headaches. We favor models with generous warranties, consistent build quality, and features that genuinely improve daily use over spec‑sheet brags that fade after the first month.
